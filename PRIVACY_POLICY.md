# Privacy Policy — ScanPilot

Last updated: November 19, 2026

ScanPilot ("the app") is developed for the US and UK Google Play market.
This policy explains what data the app collects and what it does not collect.
It is a draft for the developer to review with a lawyer before publishing;
it is not legal advice.

## Data stored on your device

- Your PDF files, scanned photos, folders, favorites and app settings are stored
  locally on your phone (Room database and the app's private storage).
- Your AI summary API key, if you enter one, is stored only on your phone
  (Android DataStore). It is never sent to the app developer.

## Data sent to other services

- **AI summary (optional, off by default):** only when you tap "AI Summary" is the
  extracted text of that one document sent to the AI service you configured in
  Settings (by default, the OpenAI-compatible endpoint and model you entered).
  No file is uploaded; only the extracted text is sent, and only for that document.
- **Nothing else is sent anywhere.** The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking
  in version 1.0. No advertising ID is collected.

## Data we do not collect

- We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or device identifiers.
- We do not sell or share your data with anyone.
- We do not access your camera or photos except when you use the scanner or
  import a file, and those photos and files stay on your device.

## Account and sign-in

- The app has no account and no sign-in. There is nothing to delete on a server,
  because no server of ours holds your data.
- To delete all your data, delete the documents inside the app or uninstall the app.

## Children

- The app is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13.
  It collects no personal data from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

- If the app changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated and the
  "last updated" date above will change.
