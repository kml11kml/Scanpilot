## Full description

ScanPilot turns your phone into a pocket scanner and PDF reader. No account, no
internet required, no ads.

Point your camera at a contract, receipt, lecture notes or ID, and get a clean PDF
in seconds. Organize documents into folders, search them, and share them with one
tap via WhatsApp, Gmail or Drive.

What is inside the app:
- Scan documents with the camera, with color, black-and-white and grayscale filters
- Read PDFs with zoom, go-to-page and night mode
- Highlight, underline, strikethrough, draw and sign on documents
- Merge several PDFs into one file and split one PDF into parts
- Convert images to PDF and PDF to images
- Lock a document with a password
- AI summaries of your documents — in plain English, in seconds
- Organize with folders, favorites and search

ScanPilot is made for:
- Students: lecture notes, articles and textbooks
- Professionals: contracts, invoices and reports
- Small businesses: receipts, quotes and paperwork
- Everyone: IDs, certificates and personal documents

Your privacy matters: files stay on your phone, and only when you tap AI Summary
is the text of that document sent to the service you chose.

Free version: unlimited reading, importing and sharing, 3 scans per day.
Pro version: unlimited scans, merge, split, AI summaries, password lock and no watermark.

Keywords: PDF scanner, scan to PDF, PDF reader, PDF editor, merge PDF, split PDF,
AI summary, document scanner, scan documents, sign PDF, PDF converter
